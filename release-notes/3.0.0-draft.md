# Remnants 3.0 (draft) — The Eclipsed Realm

Unreleased draft; the version number stays 2.7.0 in both workspaces until a release is cut.

## The Eclipsed Realm

- Added a new dimension, the Eclipsed Realm: a permanent golden dusk under a black eclipse sun, with the Gracetree burning on the northern horizon, faint stars and no clouds.
- Added six Realm biomes: the Tarnished Fields, the Gracewood, the Gravefield Barrows, the Ashen Wastes, the Stilled Sea and the underground Ossuary Depths, each with its own colours, music, ambience and decoration (graceroot trees, withered trees, gravestones, bone piles, ash, boulders and ruined pillars).
- Added the Waygate: craft an Eclipse Shard and a Waygate, raise the 3 × 4 arch, and kindle it with a shard. The arch lights rune by rune, spins up its rings and shakes the ground for six seconds, then opens onto a swirling eclipse.
- Crossing a Waygate closes the screen into a gold eclipse ring and lands you at an Arrival Shrine under the area title. The shrine's own Waygate leads back to the gate you came through.
- Added Grace Embers. Resting heals you, clears harmful effects and sets your respawn point; a trail of golden motes points toward the nearest arena; the grace menu lets you travel between every grace you have discovered or depart the Realm. Name tags rename graces. Grace shrines dot the Realm's land biomes.
- Added fog gates to the Hollow Citadel throne hall and the Gravebound Catacombs chapel: players walk through, bosses and their summons stay inside.
- Added cinematic banners: area and region titles, GRACE DISCOVERED and REMNANT FELLED.
- Added Realm building blocks: eclipse stone, cobbled eclipse stone, eclipse stone bricks (cracked, chiseled, stairs, slab, wall), graceroot logs and planks, gilded leaves and weathered gravestones.
- Added a five-step Realm advancement chain.

## Changes to existing content

- The Hollow Legion and the Cryptborn now spawn only in the Realm, from its biome spawn lists. Remnant creatures no longer spawn naturally in the overworld.
- The Hollow Citadel and the Gravebound Catacombs now generate only in the Realm (in newly generated chunks).
- The Hollow Sovereign and Gravebound Warden rituals work only in the Realm by default (`remnant/realm/realm` → `rituals_realm_only`).
- Slice 3: the Gravebound Warden, the Cryptborn (Gravebound Soldier, Crypt Lurker, Catacomb Rat, Burial Guardian), Warden gear and the Crypt Sigil replace Kotsukage, the skeleton minion, the grave skitter and the rat. Old saves migrate through the alias layer.

## Configuration

- `remnant/realm/realm`: kindle and crossing timings, crossing cooldown, arrival search radius, Realm spawn thinning and light limit, ritual lock, guidance search radius and the felled-banner radius.
- `remnant/client/presentation`: `realm_titles` and `camera_shake`.

## Verified

- Both workspaces build and pass every regression harness.
- Dedicated servers on Fabric 1.20.1, Forge 1.20.1, Fabric 1.21.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1 load the dimension, locate all six biomes, the Hollow Citadel, the Gravebound Catacombs and the grace shrines inside it, and place the Arrival Shrine.
- A scripted client walkthrough on Fabric 1.20.1 and 1.21.1 builds and kindles a Waygate, crosses, rests at the grace, tours the biomes, finds the Hollow Citadel and departs (`tools/realm-runtime/`).
- Not run: multiplayer, the Forge and NeoForge clients, and boss fights inside the Realm arenas.
