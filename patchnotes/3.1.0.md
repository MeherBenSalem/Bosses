# Remnant Bosses 3.1.0 — The Realm Endures

## Fixes

- Fixed Forge 1.20.1 not starting with `Mixin apply failed … JournalLivingEntityMixin … @Shadow field dead was not located`. This also affected the 2.7.0 and 3.0.0 Forge builds. All four builds now start normally.
- Buried Crypt Lurkers are now truly hidden until they rise. Before, they could show above the ground.
- Every Catacomb Rat now flees bright light. Before, about half of them never did.

## The Realm will not change

- Survival and adventure players can no longer break or place blocks in the Eclipsed Realm. They also cannot use items on its terrain or empty buckets there.
- Explosions still hurt, but leave the terrain whole.
- Graces, Waygates, fog gates, doors and chests still work. Creative and spectator players are exempt.
- The config key `build_protection` in `remnant/realm/realm` controls this (on by default). Items in the `#remnant_bosses:realm_usable` tag are let through.

## Graces

- Redesigned Grace Embers: a ring of weathered standing stones and a cairn on a bed of glowing ash. Above them burn a golden flame and a pool of light, with orbiting motes and a beam that marks the grace from afar.
- The grace menu lists the grace you are at first, then every other grace nearest first, each with its distance in meters. Graces in another dimension show "Another world".

## New creatures roam the Realm

- **Ashbound Constructs** are furnace-hearted machines of the Ashen Wastes, with a few in the Ossuary Depths and Tarnished Fields:
  - **Ashbound Crawler**: rears up to breathe a short cone of flame, and bursts in embers when it dies.
  - **Ember Servitor**: a hovering drone that fires burning bolts and welds damaged constructs back together.
  - **Rusted Guardian**: a slow-turning hulk with a ground smash you can jump over and a wide sweep.
- **Corrupted Relics** are sacred objects come alive in the Gracewood, Tarnished Fields, Gravefield Barrows and Ossuary Depths:
  - **Shardling**: floating crystals that take turns to fire.
  - **Ancient Vessel**: a heavy urn that slows you with a pulse when you come close, and bursts when broken.
  - **Relic Stalker**: lies hidden and invisible until you come within 7 blocks, then ambushes.
- New drops: Ashen Cog and Relic Shard. Each creature has a spawn egg.
- Constructs and relics only damage creatures. Like everything else in the Realm, they never break or place blocks.

## Existing worlds

- The Armored Grub and the Sickleghast are retired. In existing worlds they become the Ashbound Crawler and the Relic Stalker, and so do their spawn eggs.
- Existing graces get the new look on their own.

## Downloads

- Minecraft 1.20.1: Fabric and Forge; Java 17.
- Minecraft 1.21.1: Fabric and NeoForge; Java 21.
- GeckoLib and JAuml are required on all loaders. Fabric builds also require Fabric API.
