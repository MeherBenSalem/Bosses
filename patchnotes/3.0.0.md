# Remnant Bosses 3.0.0 — The Eclipsed Realm

## Explore the Eclipsed Realm

- Enter a new dimension under a black eclipse sun, with a permanent golden dusk and the Gracetree on the horizon.
- Explore seven biomes: Tarnished Fields, Gracewood, Gravefield Barrows, Ashen Wastes, Lantern Heights, Stilled Sea and Ossuary Depths.
- Craft a Waygate and kindle its arch with an Eclipse Shard. Arrive at a shrine with a Waygate home.
- Discover Grace Embers to heal, clear harmful effects, set your respawn point, follow guidance toward boss arenas and travel between discovered graces.
- Find the Hollow Citadel, Gravebound Catacombs and Forgotten Observatory. Fog gates keep bosses and their summons inside their arenas.
- Build with eclipse stone, eclipse stone bricks, graceroot wood, gilded leaves and weathered gravestones.

## Bosses, creatures and equipment

- Reworked the Hollow Sovereign with three combat phases, spectral guards, detached pauldrons and the Last Command attack.
- Added the Gravebound Warden: executioner attacks, chain grabs, grave eruptions and breakable chain-wall anchors.
- Added the Forgotten Watcher: memory-bolt volleys, a sweeping beam, lens-platform teleports, illusions and a rotating crystal aegis.
- Added the Hollow Legion, Cryptborn and Forgotten Souls creature families to the Realm.
- Added Sovereign's Ruin, Warden's Grasp, Gravebound Executioner and Memory Staff weapons; Regalia, Warden and Archivist armor; and Sovereign's Sigil, Crypt Sigil and Watcher's Eye relics.
- Added boss music, custom boss bars, area titles, grace discovery and Remnant Felled banners.
- Added a new crowned-skull eclipse icon and disabled logo blurring on Forge and NeoForge.

## Existing worlds and configuration

- Retired bosses, creatures and items resolve through registry aliases to their current successors. Existing Boss Hunts progress is preserved and renamed where appropriate.
- New arenas generate in new chunks of the Eclipsed Realm. The Hollow Legion and Cryptborn now spawn there instead of naturally spawning in the overworld.
- Added JAuml configuration for the new encounters, equipment, rituals, Realm travel, spawns and client presentation.

## Downloads

- Minecraft 1.20.1: Fabric and Forge; Java 17.
- Minecraft 1.21.1: Fabric and NeoForge; Java 21.
- GeckoLib and JAuml are required on all loaders. Fabric builds also require Fabric API.
