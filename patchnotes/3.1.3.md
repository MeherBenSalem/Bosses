# Remnant Bosses 3.1.3

## Fixes

- **The Boss Hunts Journal is readable again on Minecraft 1.21.1.** The journal's pages were drawn and then covered by
  the game's menu blur, so everything except the boss tabs and page buttons was smeared. The journal now keeps its own
  dark backdrop and the pages stay sharp. Fabric and NeoForge 1.21.1 are fixed. Forge 1.20.1 and 26.2/26.3 never
  blurred the journal, and their builds are unchanged apart from the version number.

No config or world changes.
