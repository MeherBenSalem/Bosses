# Remnant Bosses 3.1.2

## Fixes

- **No more crash when a Remnant mob holds an item that cannot be saved (Minecraft 1.21.1).** If another mod put an
  item with broken `entity_data` (no entity `id`) in a Remnant mob's hand or armor slot, for example a Burial Guardian
  in the Gravebound Catacombs, the game crashed with `Missing id for entity` the next time that chunk was saved, often
  right after a player teleported away, and threw the player back to the Overworld. Before a Remnant mob saves, such an
  item now just loses that broken data; an item that still cannot be saved is removed from the slot, and the log says
  which mob and item were affected. Fabric and NeoForge 1.21.1 are fixed. Forge 1.20.1 and 26.2/26.3 never crashed on
  this, and their builds are unchanged apart from the version number.

No config or world changes. Worlds that crashed this way load and save normally again.
