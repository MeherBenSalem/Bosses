# Remnant Bosses 3.1.1

## Fixes

- **Fog gates and Grace Embers show their textures again on NeoForge.** Their models still carried Forge's old
  `forge_data` key next to NeoForge's `neoforge_data`, and on NeoForge (1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3) both blocks could render
  as the purple and black missing texture. The Forge key is now left out of the NeoForge and Fabric builds; Forge 1.20.1
  keeps it, since Forge reads it. The blocks look as intended: the fog stays full-bright and the ember bed glows.

No gameplay, config or world changes. Modpacks that patched these two models themselves (for example with KubeJS)
can drop that patch.
